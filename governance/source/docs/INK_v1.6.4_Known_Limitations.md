# INK v1.6.4 Known Limitations

- Professional watercolor appearance remains VALIDATION REQUIRED.
- Brush materials are original procedural vector profiles, not the exact Illustrator assets shown in the tutorial.
- Paper is an independently removable overlay, not absorption or pigment interaction.
- Pigment diffusion, granulation, backruns, edge blooms, and fluid simulation are not implemented.
- Cross-host Illustrator pixel equivalence remains external validation pending.
- The current flower geometry still contributes a visibly vector-structured appearance.
