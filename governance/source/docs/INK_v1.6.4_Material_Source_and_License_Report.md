# INK v1.6.4 Material Source and License Report

The external survey was used to verify mechanisms, licensing boundaries, and candidate classes. No third-party brush artwork or paper bitmap is embedded in the candidate.

Selected runtime materials are INK project-original procedural vector profiles with embedded hashes. The procedural paper layer is an independent overlay and is not described as paper absorption or physical interaction.

External references remain in `assets/watercolor-v2/MATERIAL_SOURCE_AND_LICENSE_MANIFEST.json` with their admission decision. Unknown Dagubi and VariousFree assets observed in the tutorial remain excluded because their asset files and redistribution rights are unresolved.

The embedded selected assets are:

- Soft-edge Petal Brush v2
- Broken-edge Petal Brush v2
- Dense Pigment Brush v2
- Leaf Wash Brush v2
- Tapered Stem Brush v2
- Irregular Vector Wash v2
- Natural Splatter Field v2
- Procedural Paper Overlay v1
