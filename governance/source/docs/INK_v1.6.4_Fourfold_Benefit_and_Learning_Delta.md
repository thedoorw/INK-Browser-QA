# INK v1.6.4 Fourfold Benefit and Learning Delta

## INK Capability Increment

Brush Material v2, deterministic irregular stroke profiles, layered pigment concentration, directional leaves and stems, non-rectangular wash, non-uniform splatter, separable paper overlay, Stable ID, local replacement, replay, rollback, and deterministic SVG/PNG.

## Capability Gap Increment

Exact Illustrator brush artwork, brush-internal raster dependency, real paper interaction, pigment diffusion, granulation, fluid simulation, and Illustrator pixel ground truth remain unsupported or externally pending.

## Behavior Increment

Eight Atomic, six Composite, and seven Strategy behavior candidates are stored under `INTELLIGENCE_CANDIDATES/Watercolor_Behaviors`.

## Material and Structure Increment

Ten material/structure candidates are stored under `INTELLIGENCE_CANDIDATES/Watercolor_Materials` and `Watercolor_Structures`.

## Learning Delta

Before v1.6.4, INK could execute editable vector-watercolor structure but relied heavily on regular transparent shapes. After v1.6.4, INK can choose profiled materials, introduce controlled deterministic irregularity, create local pigment concentration, use directional botanical strokes, and keep wash, splatter, and paper overlay independently editable. Visual evidence remains below the professional watercolor reference, so the intelligence remains candidate-level.
