# Learning Delta

## Before
Before Batch 02, verified behavior was concentrated on isolated templates, radial rings, simple multi-layer flowers and a single plant unit. Complex viewpoint, complete composition, complex JSX translation and real watercolor procedure boundaries were not jointly validated.

## After
After Batch 02, AI can select and assemble high-density multi-ring flowers, non-front flower structures, branched plant hierarchies and a complete botanical poster; it can quantify complex JSX translation and define the exact raster boundary of a real Adobe watercolor procedure while preserving unaffected objects during local edits.
